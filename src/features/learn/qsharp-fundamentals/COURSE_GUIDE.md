# Q# Fundamentals — Course Guide

Welcome to **Q# Fundamentals**! This course introduces Microsoft's Q# language for quantum computing, covering program structure, operations, functions, qubit allocation, and measurement.

## Course Overview
- **Chapter 1: Introduction to Q# & Quantum Environment**
  - Lesson 0: What is Q#?
  - Lesson 1: Operations vs Functions
  - Lesson 2: Allocating Qubits (`use`)
- **Chapter 2: Q# Variables, Types & Control Flow**
  - Lesson 3: Immutable & Mutable Variables (`let` & `mutable`)
  - Lesson 4: Q# Primitive & Quantum Types
  - Lesson 5: Control Flow (`if`, `for`, `while`)
- **Chapter 3: Measurement & Classical Interoperability**
  - Lesson 6: Measuring Qubits (`M` operation)
  - Lesson 7: Resetting & Releasing Qubits (`Reset`)
  - Lesson 8: Returning Classical Results
